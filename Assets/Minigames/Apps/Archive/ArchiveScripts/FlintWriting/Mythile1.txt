Dear Bundle of Pulped Trees,

Yesterday, when I tried to go to my usual appointment with the shrink, I was given you instead of the medical care I've grown to expect. The receptionist told me that 'due to recent staffing shortages, my concerning dreams no longer qualify me for treatment.' To clarify, as you might expect, 'concerning dreams' was spoken with the most condescending tone she could muster, as if those dreams hadn't caused me enough stress to start losing feathers and start needing anxiety medication.

So, instead of professional healthcare, I have you, a dream journal, a notebook one of the other staff members gave me out of pity.

Whatever.

It's not the first time I've been requested to write in one of these, but it will be the first time I've been able to be completely honest while doing so. Ministry therapists can never be trusted to keep their confidentiality when it comes to blasphemy, and my dreams contain plenty of that.

Anyways, I can only vent for so long before this becomes a normal diary instead of a dream journal, so lets start with last night's dream, one I've repeated many many times... The twin dream...

----

The dream starts with a few blurry and distant faces looking out at me from a tall, branchy building. Most of the faces look similar to my own, something I would normally find unnerving, but their expressions feel genuinely friendly.

When I first started having this dream, I would often try to enter the building they were all in, even getting cheered on to join them inside, but would find that every window and door wouldn't budge to let me in... I tend to not linger on this part of the dream as it only ever serves to make me sad...

Anyways, there is only one other building in the scarcely populated dream landscape, a theater, and no matter what, I always feel myself forced to walk there eventually. And right as I grab the door handle to enter, I find myself teleported to the center of a stage, forced to stare down at a vast audience. However, instead of row after row of audience filled chairs, the entire floor space is filled with a massive cityscape, millions of people watching me and judging me. And worst of all, way up on the balcony, the silhouette of the theater manager watches me, serpentine eyes constantly annoyed.

I know he's judging my performance, and I know I need the audience to love me if I don't want to suffer his wrath...

I have no script, so I just improv, trying to sing, dance, or even write poetry, and I know all of it's awful. But, despite the ill conceived prose I spew out, the audience still gives me these grim, barely audible cheers. The sound leaves a pit in my stomach that's hard to describe... I think silence would have been kinder as the noise reminds me of people forcing themselves to have a good time and failing miserably.

I keep looking up to the balcony, hoping the meager cheers would be good enough, but each time the silhouette looks away, disappointed. I know that I need to move this audience, and that the sounds of pity and fear would never be enough for the theater manager.

The performance always ends with hushed applause, and I exit the stage right, into a blindingly white room.

Inside the room, I only find part of the manager waiting for me, just his shadow. The shade lingers on the wall, just as long and imposing as the real serpent was. I try to argue with the figure, but my beak can only form sad peeps, no matter how much I try to shout with rage. The shade only responds with a smug grin cut into his visage, as if it enjoys seeing me this upset. I know I want to punch the bastard in the face, but know I would only be punching the theater wall.

The shade waits for me to quiet myself before informing me that the next performance would be beginning immediately, pointing me to the door. And right before I leave, he forces a script into my talons. I leave, anxiously throwing the script away the moment I can't see the shade anymore.

These two scenes keep repeating themselves in a cycle, each time feeling more and more like a prison that I can't escape. Usually I wake up in a cold sweat, after three or so iterations, my anxiety so elevated that I feel like I'm about to have a heart attack, my limbs spasming and tingling numb as I try to steady myself. 

But on rare occasions, I sleep long enough for the cycle to break...

On those occasions, I near the end of my performance, trying to dance as hard as I can, when I trip, falling forward, threatening to crush the audience. I close my eyes to avoid seeing the horror of what is to come, only for the crash to never arrive. I open my eyes and look up, seeing a fake of myself back up on the stage, once more singing and dancing. As for the real me, I'm a member of the audience now, surrounded by a lively city that barely watches fake me perform for his life. I suddenly have much less anxiety, but much more fear.

I don't know what it all means, but I know one thing. This dream acted as a prophecy: I saw my fake on stage before I saw him in the sky. What are the chances of that? And I know that there is truth to dig for beyond that, like how vulnerable the gods are, or how there are even greater things above them...

I need others to know. We can't fall in line before these false gods. We need to act on the truth. I think I'm going to contact Closed Sky and see if they can help as I'm sure the ministry won't.

Hey, writing out my dreams does give clarity, doesn't it?

-Mythile
