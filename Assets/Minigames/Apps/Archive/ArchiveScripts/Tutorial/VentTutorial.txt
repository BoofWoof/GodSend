<color=red><size=45><b>Quick Start Guide:</b></size></color>

For proper purifier operations, insure that every vent intake leads to at least one purification station while avoiding any leaks along the way.

<color=red><size=45><b>Detailed Guide:</b></size></color>

<b>Timer:</b>
If the purifier system shows a countdown, this is the estimate of time left before local fume density raises to the point where hallucinogenic effects exceed healthy limits and can cause long term mental damage. It is strongly recommended to finalize vent formation before this timer is depleted.

<b>Consequences:</b>
Failure to beat the timer will not kill you. It will simply damage your city's sanity and temporarily reduce efficiency. However, there is some good news. This should be enough time for the city's assist system to kick in to provide better route analysis. If the assist system shows a route as green, the route is good. If the assist system shows a route as red, the route either has a leak or does not reach a purifier.

<b>Multiples:</b>
Some intakes will be split to feed into two or more vents. These are powerful intakes that take in more fumes than typical and thus require every feed to lead to a purifier.
